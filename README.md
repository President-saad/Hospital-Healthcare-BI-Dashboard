# Hospital & Healthcare BI Dashboard

## Overview
Business Intelligence Dashboard analyzing hospital and 
healthcare data using Python, Pandas and Power BI to 
uncover patient trends, medical conditions, billing 
patterns and admission insights.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Power BI
- JupyterLab

## Questions Answered
- How many patients were admitted?
- What are the most common medical conditions?
- What is the average billing amount?
- What is the average length of stay?
- Which blood type is most common?
- What is the breakdown of admission types?
- What are the most common test results?

## Key Findings
- A total of 55,392 patients were admitted across 
  all hospitals in the dataset.

- Arthritis was the most common medical condition 
  with 9,297 patients followed by Diabetes 
  with 9,284 patients.

- The average billing amount per patient was 
  $25,590.08 showing significant healthcare costs.

- The average length of stay was 15.5 days 
  per patient.

- Gender distribution was almost equal with 
  27,726 Male and 27,666 Female patients.

- Blood types were evenly distributed across 
  all patients with A- being the most common.

- Elective admissions were the most common 
  type with 18,618 cases followed by Urgent 
  with 18,538 cases.

- Test results were almost evenly split between 
  Abnormal (18,589), Normal (18,488) and 
  Inconclusive (18,315).

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive slicers for Gender, Admission Type 
  and Medical Condition
- KPI Cards showing Total Patients, Average 
  Billing Amount and Average Length of Stay
- Multiple charts showing patient demographics 
  and medical trends
